The [Famicom](../../wiki/Family_Computer/) had the capability of audio expansion, via an audio return loop on its [cartridge connector](../../wiki/Cartridge_connector/). Cartridges containing additional hardware would mix the [incoming 2A03 audio](../../wiki/APU/) signal with their own audio signal produced by the [mapper](../../wiki/Mapper/) chip. The [FDS](../../wiki/Family_Computer_Disk_System/) also produced expansion sound which operated through the same cartridge connector.
