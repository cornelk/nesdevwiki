These mapper numbers are worthless. Either:

- They were assigned to hardware that isn't useful in isolation (e.g. [FDS BIOS](../../wiki/FDS_BIOS/), [Fukutake Study Box](../../wiki/Fukutake_Study_Box/), [Game Genie](../../wiki/Game_Genie/), etc.)
- They're duplicates of other mappers, or
- They were assigned to represent hardware that doesn't exist.
