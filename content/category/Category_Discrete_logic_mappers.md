Discrete logic [mappers](../../wiki/Mapper/) are made almost entirely using stock components, especially the commonly available [74-series chips](../7400_series/). More often than not, discrete mappers are susceptible to [bus conflicts](../Mappers_with_bus_conflicts/).

See User:Lidnariq/Discrete Logic Table, User:Lidnariq/MMC3 Variants#Discrete logic mappers and [Comparison of Nintendo mappers#Discrete_logic](../../wiki/Comparison_of_Nintendo_mappers/#Discrete_logic) for alternative ways to compare these mappers.
