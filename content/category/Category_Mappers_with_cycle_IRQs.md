Mappers in this category have the ability to schedule an IRQ an arbitrary and continuously-adjustable number of CPU cycles in the future. Cycle counting mappers that have a fixed interval that cannot be configured belong in the [fixed-timing subcategory](../Mappers_with_fixed-timing_cycle_IRQs/).
