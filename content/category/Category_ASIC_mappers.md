ASIC [mappers](../../wiki/Mapper/) are, as the name suggests, [custom-made integrated circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application-specific_integrated_circuit) used to perform memory mapping tasks that are too complex or cost-prohibitive to implement using discrete components. Many ASIC mappers also include additional features such as interrupt counters, nametable [mirroring](../../wiki/Mirroring/) controls, and sometimes even additional sound channels.
