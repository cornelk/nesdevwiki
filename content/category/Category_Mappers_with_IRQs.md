Many boards have the ability to tell the CPU that it's time to do something specific. See [IRQ](../../wiki/IRQ/).

- [Category:Mappers with fixed-timing IRQs](../Mappers_with_fixed-timing_IRQs/) - IRQs that do not have a configurable timing interval.
- [Category:Mappers with cycle IRQs](../Mappers_with_cycle_IRQs/) - IRQs that operate by counting CPU cycles.
- [Category:Mappers with scanline IRQs](../Mappers_with_scanline_IRQs/) - IRQs that operate in response to the PPU for scanline-based timing.
