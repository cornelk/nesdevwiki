This category consists of mappers whose primary purpose was for [multicarts](../../wiki/Multicart/). (CNROM and most MMC1 boards don't count, despite *Donkey Kong Classics* and *Super Mario Bros./Duck Hunt/World Class Track Meet*.) Most of these were only ever released with one set of games.
