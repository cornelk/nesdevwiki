This mapper is used in Yuxing's ROMs. PRG ROM=1024KB,CHR ROM=0KB. see [yxleimeng.net](http://www.yxleimeng.net)(chinese)

For some reason, GoodNES used this mapper for the singleton multicart [Contra 168-in-1](http://community.fandom.com/wiki/c:bootleggames:Contra_168_in_1), but that seems to be identical to [iNES Mapper 015](../INES_Mapper_015/).
