**NES 2.0 Mapper 410** is a variant of [mapper 45](../INES_Mapper_045/) where the ASIC's PRG A21/CHR A20 output (set by bit 6 of the [third write to \$6000](../INES_Mapper_045/#CHR-AND.2C_CHR-OR.2FPRG-OR_MSB_.28.246000_.232.2C_write.29)) selects between regularly-banked CHR-ROM (=0) and 8 KiB of unbanked CHR-RAM (=1). It is used solely for the *Super 8-in-1 - 98格鬥天王＋熱血* (JY-302) multicart.
