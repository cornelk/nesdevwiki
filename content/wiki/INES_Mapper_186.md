**iNES Mapper 186** was originally assigned for the BIOS of the [Fukutake Study Box](../Fukutake_Study_Box/). The only emulators that emulate this device, Mesen and NintendulatorNRS, both require the BIOS image as a headerless .BIN file however, making this mapper assignment useless.
