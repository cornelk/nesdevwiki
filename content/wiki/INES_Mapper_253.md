iNES Mapper 253 is used for Waixing's Chinese localization of the Japanese Bandai game *Dragon Ball Z: 強襲! サイヤ人 (Dragon Ball Z: Kyōshū! Saiya-jin)*. It uses a clone of the Konami [VRC4](../VRC2_and_VRC4/), with the chip's A0 connected to CPU A2 and the chip's A1 connected to CPU A3 (VRC4e, Mapper 23 submapper 2). It has 8 KiB of PRG-RAM at CPU \$6000-\$7FFF, and 1 KiB CHR pages 4 and 5 are CHR-RAM.

[INES Mapper 252](../INES_Mapper_252/) is the same, except that it's 1 KiB CHR pages 6 and 7 that are CHR-RAM.

Mappers [74](../INES_Mapper_074/), [192](../INES_Mapper_192/), [194](../INES_Mapper_194/), and [195](../INES_Mapper_195/), also used for Waixing's localizations, are similar but use an MMC3.
