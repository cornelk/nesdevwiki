Nestopia's source claims that iNES Mapper 054 was allocated for the board containing the multicart *Novel Diamond 9999999-in-1*. However, its implementation is what Disch's notes say for [iNES Mapper 201](../INES_Mapper_201/).

(In turn, Nestopia's implementation of mapper 201 is halfway between what Disch's notes say for m201 and [iNES Mapper 226](../INES_Mapper_226/))

GoodNES 3.1.4 and FCEUX 2.2.2 have nothing allocated to mapper 54, and only have Novel Diamond as a [UNIF](../UNIF/) dump with MAPR "BMC-NovelDiamond9999999in1".
