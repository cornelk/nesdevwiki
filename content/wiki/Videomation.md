***Videomation*** is a paint program for NES using the [CPROM](../CPROM/) board, published by THQ in 1991.

It can be seen as the spiritual predecessor of *Mario Paint*. It has no save feature; the extra battery-backed RAM that would be needed was seen as an unnecessary cost in the VHS era. It has no [mouse](../Mouse/) support, but instead a button can be pressed to toggle among various cursor speeds (much as in *Image Fight*), and one of the pens has "race car" style control. Animation is with one of eight predefined sprites, and music depends on the animation. The fill tool is extremely slow.
