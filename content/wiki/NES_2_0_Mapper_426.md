**NES 2.0 Mapper 426** denotes VT369-based handheld plug 'n play consoles that connect a 256-byte serial ROM to the VT369's GPIO port at \$4150. The serial ROM's data is included as NES 2.0 Misc. ROM at the end of the ROM image file.
