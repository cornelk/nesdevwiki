The **74688** is an 8-bit identity comparator 74-series logic IC. This IC is useful for decoding one specific and fine range of address.

### Pinout {#Pinout}

    74688 Pinout  

                             .--__--. 
                        /CE -|01  20|- VCC 
                         A0 -|02  19|- /A=B 
                         B0 -|03  18|- B7 
                         A1 -|04  17|- A7 
                         B1 -|05  16|- B6 
                         A2 -|06  15|- A6 
                         B2 -|07  14|- B5 
                         A3 -|08  13|- A5 
                         B3 -|09  12|- B4 
                        GND -|10  11|- A4 
                             '------' 

### Signal descriptions {#Signal_descriptions}

- A0-A7 : 8-bit content A
- B0-B7 : 8-bit content B
- /A=B : low when the 8-bit contents of A and B are identical
- /CE : /A=B pin is forced to output high when this pin is 1
- VCC, GND : power supply
