NES 2.0 Mapper 527 is used for a bootleg version of Taito's 不動明王伝 (Fudō Myōō Den). Its UNIF board name is **UNL-AX-40G**. The original [INES Mapper 207](../INES_Mapper_207/) is replaced with a [VRC2](../VRC2_and_VRC4/) clone (A0/A1, i.e. VRC2b) while retaining Mapper 207's extended mirroring. Accordingly, CHR A17 is connected to CIRAM A10.
