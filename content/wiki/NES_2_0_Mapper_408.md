**NES 2.0 Mapper 408** was implemented for the plug & play version of *Konami Collector's Series: Arcade Advanced*. It works similarly to [Mapper 256](../NES_2.0_Mapper_256/) but switches between CHR-ROM and CHR-RAM by repurposing higher address lines.

A non-exhaustive list of other notable, bootleg consoles that use it:

- *Super Mini SFC*
- *G6*
