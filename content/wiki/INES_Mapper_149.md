iNES Mapper 149 is just like mapper [145](../INES_Mapper_145/), but moves the register to overlap with PRG ROM and so has bus conflicts. It's a singleton, PCB labeled SA-0036, and used by the Sachen game *Taiwan Mahjong 16*.
