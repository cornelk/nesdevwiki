**NES 2.0 mapper 329** denotes the Russian *Keyboard Transformer* cartridge that includes Family BASIC modified to run with an IBM PC/AT keyboard connected to the cartridge. This represents UNIF MAPR **UNL-Transformer**.

NES 2.0 Mapper 329 was originally defined as [iNES Mapper 177](../INES_Mapper_177/) with more than 8 KiB of PRG-RAM for *Мооспирв обучающий компьютер 2000*, but this is redundant with NES 2.0's CHR-RAM size field.
