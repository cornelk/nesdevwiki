NES 2.0 Mapper 367 denotes a *7-in-1* multicart using an unknown PCB ID that constitutes a variant of [INES Mapper 205](../INES_Mapper_205/). It latches the *address* bits A0 and A1 instead of the *data* bits D0 and D1 when address range \$6000-\$7FFF is written to, and is otherwise identical to mapper 205.
