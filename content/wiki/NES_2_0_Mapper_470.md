**NES 2.0 Mapper 470** denotes the **INX_007T_V01** multicart circuit board, used for the Retro-Bit re-release of *Battletoads and Double Dragon*. It is basically [AOROM](../AxROM/) with an additional outer bank register at \$5000-\$5FFF whose data selects the 256 KiB outer bank. The outer bank register is cleared to \$00 on reset.
