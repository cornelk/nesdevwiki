The **KrzysioCart** (Chris' MicroSD Cartridge) is a CPLD and SD card based [Flash Cartridge](../../category/Flash_Cartridge/) made by krzysiobal. It is intended to be a lower budget flash cartridge compared to the [Everdrive](../Everdrive_N8/). The cartridge targets the most commonly used mappers, and supports about 82% of the NES / Famicom game library. [\[1\]](https://forums.nesdev.org/viewtopic.php?t=15757)

Specifications:

- PRG-ROM size: 1 MB
- CHR size: 256 KB
- PRG-RAM size: 8 KB

Product Links:

- Website: <http://krzysiobal.com/krzysiocart/>
- Nesdev Forum Topic: <https://forums.nesdev.org/viewtopic.php?t=15757>
- Ebay: <https://www.ebay.com/itm/392943601934>
