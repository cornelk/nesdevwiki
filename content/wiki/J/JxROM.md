The generic designation **JxROM** refers to cartridge boards made by Nintendo of Europe that use the [Sunsoft FME-7](../Sunsoft_FME-7/) mapper IC. These boards are the European counterpart to boards manufactured by Sunsoft that used the FME-7, including several Famicom boards and the North American **NES-BTR**. The [iNES](../INES/) format assigns [Mapper 69](../Sunsoft_FME-7/) to JxROM.

Nintendo manufactured these JxROM boards for the European market:

| Board | PRG ROM | PRG RAM            | CHR         | comments          |
|-------|---------|--------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| JLROM | 256 KiB |                    | 128 KiB ROM |                   |
| JSROM | 128 KiB | 8 KiB (no battery) | 256 KiB ROM | equivalent to BTR |
