Mapper 248 was mistakenly assigned to some Chinese games, for instance, Bao Qing Tian. [iNES Mapper 115](../INES_Mapper_115/), when implemented correctly, will handle this game, and should render Mapper 248's assignment obsolete.

It has been proposed in \#nesdev that mapper 248 should be reassigned for private use for mappers still in development, such as in emulators and in the [PowerPak](../PowerPak/).
