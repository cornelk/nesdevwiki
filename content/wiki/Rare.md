**Rare Ltd.** is a video game studio founded by Tim and Chris Stamper. Many of their games used [AxROM](../AxROM/) boards. Several games by Rare, such as *Battletoads*, are harsh [tests for emulators](../Tricky-to-emulate_games/) because they depend on so many NES timing quirks.

## After the NES era {#After_the_NES_era}

From 1994 to 2003, Rare developed games like *GoldenEye 007*, *Banjo-Kazooie*, and *Perfect Dark* exclusively for Nintendo platforms. In 2003, Microsoft bought Rare.
