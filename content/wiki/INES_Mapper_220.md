iNES Mapper 220 is used by FCEUX as a debugging mapper assignment. Although it normally shouldn't be assigned to ROMs for regular usage, several iNES ROM images online are set to Mapper 220, including [*Gyruss* (FDS conversion)](../NES_2.0_Mapper_302/) and [*Boogerman II: The Final Adventure*](../NES_2.0_Mapper_263/). Emulators should perform a hash check on the ROM image and compare the database to determine the correct mapper being used.
