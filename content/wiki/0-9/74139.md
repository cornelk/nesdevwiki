The **74139** (common variants 74LS139, 74HC139) is a dual 2-to-4-line decoder/demultiplexer in the 7400 series. Each side of the chip is a decoder with one active low enable from a 2-bit address to four active low signals. The two decoders can be wired independently, or a `/Y` from one decoder can be wired to the `/EN` on the other to calculate more complex combinational logic functions.

## 74139 Pinout {#74139_Pinout}

            .---v---.
    A /EN -o|1    16|-- VCC
     A S0 --|2    15|o- B /EN
     A S1 --|3    14|-- B S0
    A /Y0 -o|4    13|-- B S1
    A /Y1 -o|5    12|o- B /Y0
    A /Y2 -o|6    11|o- B /Y1
    A /Y3 -o|7    10|o- B /Y2
      GND --|8     9|o- B /Y3
            `-------'

## Signal descriptions {#Signal_descriptions}

The chip includes two independent decoders.

- VCC, GND : power supply
- S0, S1 : select which /Yx line to pull low
- /EN : force all /Yx outputs to be high when this pin is high
- /Y0-/Y3 : output signals
