The 74138 (common variants 74LS138, 74HC138) is a 3-to-8-line decoder/demultiplexer in the 7400 series. The decoder has one active high and two active low enables and is often used to calculate complex boolean functions.

## 74138 Pinout {#74138_Pinout}

           .---v---.
      A0 --|1    16|-- VCC
      A1 --|2    15|o- /Y0
      A2 --|3    14|o- /Y1
    /EN1 -o|4    13|o- /Y2
    /EN2 -o|5    12|o- /Y3
     EN3 --|6    11|o- /Y4
     /Y7 -o|7    10|o- /Y5
     GND --|8     9|o- /Y6
           `-------'

## Signal descriptions {#Signal_descriptions}

It computes the following function:

- A0-A2 : select which /Yx line to pull low
- /EN1, /EN2, EN3 : if they aren't respectively 0, 0 and 1, the all outputs of /Yx pins will be forced to high
- /Y0-/Y7 : output signals
- VCC, GND : power supply
