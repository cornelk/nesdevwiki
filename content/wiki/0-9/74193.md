The **74193** is a 74-series synchronous 4-bit binary up/down counter.

## Pinout {#Pinout}

    74193 Pinout  

                              .--__--. 
                         D1 ->|01  16|-- VCC 
                         Q1 <-|02  15|<- D0 
                         Q0 <-|03  14|<- +Clear 
                       Down ->|04  13|-> Borrow Out 
                         Up ->|05  12|-> Carry Out 
                         Q2 <-|06  11|<- /Load
                         Q3 <-|07  10|<- D2 
                        GND --|08  09|<- D3 
                              '------' 

## Signal descriptions {#Signal_descriptions}

- VCC, GND: power supply
- D0-D3: data inputs
- Q0-Q3: counter outputs
- /Load: copy the current content of D to Q when this pin is low
- Up, Down: count up or down the Q at the **rising edge** of these pins
- +Clear: set all Q contents to 0 when this pin is high
- Carry Out, Borrow Out: **normally high**, becomes low when the counter was at 15 (Carry Out) or 0 (Borrow Out) and the **falling edge** of Up/Down happens, **stays low** until the **rising edge** of Up/Down
