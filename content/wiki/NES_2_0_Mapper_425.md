**NES 2.0 Mapper 425** denotes the circuit board used for [VT369](../VTxx/)-based handheld plug'n play consoles from Cube Tech. It extends the [VT369](../VTxx/)'s 32 MiB address space to 128 MiB through an outer bank register at \$41E6.
