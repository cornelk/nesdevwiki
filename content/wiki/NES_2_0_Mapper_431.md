**NES 2.0 Mapper 431** was assigned for the Realtec **GN-91B** multicart. However, it turned out to cover the same functionality as [NES 2.0 Mapper 396](../NES_2.0_Mapper_396/), which was initially designated solely for the 晶太 YY850437C.
