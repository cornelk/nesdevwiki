\$4017 is an [APU](../APU/) or [Joypad](../Controller_reading/) register.

- When writing to this address, it affects the [APU Frame Counter](../APU/#Frame_Counter_.28.244017.29).
- When reading from this address, it [reads Player 2's controller.](../Controller_reading/#.244016_.2F_.244017_Read)
