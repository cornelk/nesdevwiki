**NES 2.0 Mapper 374** denotes reset-based multicarts around the [MMC1](../MMC1/) ASIC. When an interruption of the M2 signal is detected, the higher address lines are automatically incremented. So far, only 128 KiB PRG+128 KiB CHR size variants have been found:

- *1995 Super HiK 4-in-1 - 新系列機器戰警组合卡 (JY-022)*
- *1996 Super HiK 4-in-1 - 新系列超級飛狼組合卡 (JY-051)*
