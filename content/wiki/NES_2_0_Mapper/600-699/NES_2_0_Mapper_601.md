**NES 2.0 Mapper 601** denotes UM6578-based plug-and-play consoles from dreamGEAR and Scenario that extend the addressable PRG-ROM from 1 to 2 MiB by switching A20 for the entire address range via \$4026 bit 7. Used by:

- *dreamGEAR Plug 'N' Play꞉ 25 Classic & Racing Games* (DGUN-806)
- *Senario Card & Casino Games 10 Games in 1*
- *Senario Vs. Maxx꞉ 25 Classic & Racing Games*
