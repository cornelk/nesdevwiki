**NES 2.0 Mapper 423** denotes the circuit board used for the *Lexibook Compact Cyber Arcade* handheld plug'n play consoles. It extends the [VT369](../VTxx/)'s 32 MiB address space to 64 MiB through an outer bank register at \$4150.
