NES 2.0 Mapper 532 is used for a Chinese translation hack of *Sangokushi II - Haou no Tairiku* which uses the VirtuaNES EX emulator to overlay Chinese text onto the game's graphics. The UNIF MAPR designation is "CHINA_ER_SAN2"
