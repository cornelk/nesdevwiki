NES 2.0 Mapper 517 is used for 까치와 노래친구 (Kkachi-wa Nolae Chingu), a Korean Karaoke game. It uses banking similar to [UNROM](../UxROM/) without bus conflicts, but has additional ADC hardware (different from [NES 2.0 Mapper 515](../NES_2.0_Mapper_515/)) for its custom microphone socket at \$6000 and \$6001.
