**NES 2.0 Mapper 433** denotes the **NC-20MB** PCB, used for the *20-in-1 (CA-006)* multicart. It is almost identical to [INES Mapper 233](../INES_Mapper_233/), except that mirroring is selected just by single bit 6 (1=Horizontal).
