**NES 2.0 Mapper 469** denotes BlazePro multicarts consisting of games originally released for the Famicom Disk System. The circuit board is physically identical to [NES 2.0 Mapper 468](../NES_2.0_Mapper_468/), but the CPLD is programmed differently.
